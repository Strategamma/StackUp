How to StackUp

Choose togetherSelect one card, then Lock in. Everyone reveals at the same time.
Stack downwardYour card goes after the closest larger ending—like a smaller plate on a larger plate.
Oversized cardIf your card is larger than every ending, choose one stack to collect.
Avoid the red XThe X marks slot six. Playing there makes you collect the previous five cards.
Skull penalties: normal 1 · multiples of 5 = 2 · multiples of 10 = 3 · doubles (11, 22…) = 5 · card 55 = 7

Cards resolve highest to lowest. After 10 rounds, the player with the fewest skulls wins.
